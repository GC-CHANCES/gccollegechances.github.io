# GC College Chances

A free college-admissions planner for Greenfield-Central High School students in Greenfield, Indiana.
Pick your GC courses, activities, and sports, enter your GPA and SAT, and see estimated
admission chances at 30 colleges, plus the Indiana honors diploma checklist for your class.

Made for students. Not an official Greenfield-Central Community Schools site, and not
endorsed by any college. Estimates only; talk with your GC counselor.

The page is a single file, `index.html`. Its source lives in the `gc-admissions/` folder of
the CONRAD project, and `build-site.py` there produces this file.
