Class of 2025

Where GC graduates went, from the school's postgraduate profile.

GC grading

  • Weighting: AP and approved dual credit add 0.5 with a C- or better. An A is 4.0, or 4.5 weighted.
  • No class rank since the Class of 2025.
  • Latin Honors: Summa Cum Laude 4.0+, Magna 3.8–3.9, Cum Laude 3.6–3.7 (PowerSchool GPA, not rounded).
  • Retakes: a D+ or lower can be retaken once; the higher grade counts.

Dual credit

  • IU ACP: 2.7 GPA needed for college credit.
  • Ball State early childhood: 3.0 GPA.
  • Ivy Tech: be program ready, for example SAT 460 Reading & Writing or a 2.6 GPA with Core 40 after four semesters. Digital Electronics also needs SAT 510 Math.

Senior year

  1. Junior springSAT at school. It counts.
  2. Early OctIndiana Pre-Admissions letter lists colleges that pre-admit you.
  3. Oct 1FAFSA opens.
  4. Nov 1Early action at IU and Purdue; Notre Dame Restrictive Early Action.
  5. Apr 15Indiana FAFSA deadline for state aid and 21st Century Scholars.
